This casebook collects applications of microeconomics and game theory. It is being developed by Isogai, who plans to expand the materials through their use in student supervision, including seminars.

The collection includes cases at the idea stage. Parts of the text, applications, and references are AI-drafted, sources not yet verified. Demo values and data are fictional teaching materials.
