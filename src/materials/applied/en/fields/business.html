Explore choices by firms and customers through pricing, two-sided markets, and contracts. Models with explicit assumptions help identify the conditions relevant to a decision.

Idea: designing a menu of prices

How do subscriptions and usage-based prices affect purchases and usage? We envisage a teaching example using fictional customer types to explore price discrimination and choice.

Idea: pricing and participation in two-sided markets

On a service connecting users and sellers, how do fees on one side affect the other? We are considering a small model in which students can vary the interdependence of participation.

Idea: contracts and incentives

How does performance-related pay affect effort and risk bearing? The idea is to use fictional examples to explore the basics of contracts under asymmetric information.
