Communication and information

Contents

  1. Idea: information disclosure and communication
  2. Idea: conflicting interests and advice

Explore how communication and the presentation of information affect decisions. Possible topics include information design, cheap talk, and information provision on platforms.

Idea: information disclosure and communication

Use small examples to compare information disclosure and receivers’ decisions. This would provide an introduction to Kamenica–Gentzkow (2011), “Bayesian Persuasion.”

Idea: conflicting interests and advice

Explore how differences in the sender’s and receiver’s objectives affect communication. We envisage a case based on Crawford–Sobel (1982), “Strategic Information Transmission.”

All applications and references are AI-drafted, sources not yet verified. A demo and seminar activities are topics for future consideration.
