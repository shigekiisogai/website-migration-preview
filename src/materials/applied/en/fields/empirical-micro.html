Idea: mergers and price effects

Combine demand estimation with firms’ pricing decisions to compare a fictional market before and after a merger. We envisage teaching materials that distinguish changes in costs from changes in competition.

Idea: store survival and entry

Explore entry and exit through changes in local demand and fixed costs. Topics to consider include data comparing the same area over time and the distinction between static and dynamic analysis.
