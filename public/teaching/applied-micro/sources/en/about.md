# About this casebook

This casebook collects applications of microeconomics and game theory. It is being developed by Isogai, who plans to expand the materials through their use in student supervision, including seminars.

Isogai's research fields are **microeconomics, game theory, and industrial organization**. Isogai is currently preparing the texts and cases independently. Student participation is envisaged for next semester at the earliest, or possibly the end of the current semester.

## Three reading levels

Start with **★1 Intuition** for an introduction through visuals and hands-on exploration. Choose **★2 Model** to examine simple models, or **★3 Research** to explore papers, estimation methods, and possible seminar or thesis questions. You can switch levels at the top of each case page.

## Case status and future development

Each case displays a stage: Idea, Planning, Done in seminar, or Outcomes. All three current cases are at the Idea stage. Activity records will be added after dates, content, and shareable outcomes have been confirmed. Students' personal information will remain private.

For future updates, we are considering a workflow in which students write cases in Markdown and an instructor reviews them. Markdown files editable in Obsidian serve as the source documents.

## Verification status of this prototype

**Text, applications, and references: AI-drafted, sources not yet verified**. Computational checks of the demos and verification against academic sources are recorded separately. Numerical values and maps are fictional teaching data. They do not represent actual firms, places, or student achievements.

## Data and terms of use

The fictional preference rankings, assumed prices and quality, grid populations, and choices generated from a fixed seed were newly created for this prototype. **These fictional data are provided under CC0 1.0.** Each case describes how they are generated.

The publication license for the text and program as a whole will be decided before publication. e-Stat and National Land Numerical Information are possible future data sources. Terms of use and attribution will be checked for each dataset adopted.

This prototype loads local resources. It saves language and reading-level preferences in the browser's localStorage. It has no forms collecting personal information or access analytics.
