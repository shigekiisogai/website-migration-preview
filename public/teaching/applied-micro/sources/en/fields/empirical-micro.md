# Empirical microeconomics

Use data and models to examine demand, prices, and firm entry and exit. Carefully connect observed relationships to predictions under explicit assumptions.

## Case study

Start with [Demand Prediction and Estimation with Discrete Choice Models](../cases/discrete-choice-logit.md) and the [Location Choice and Entry Games](../cases/location-entry-game.md).

## Idea: mergers and price effects

Combine demand estimation with firms' pricing decisions to compare a fictional market before and after a merger.
We envisage teaching materials that distinguish changes in costs from changes in competition.

## Idea: store survival and entry

Explore entry and exit through changes in local demand and fixed costs.
Topics to consider include data comparing the same area over time and the distinction between static and dynamic analysis.
