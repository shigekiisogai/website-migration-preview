# Communication and information

Explore how communication and the presentation of information affect decisions. Possible topics include information design, cheap talk, and information provision on platforms.

## Idea: information disclosure and communication

Use small examples to compare information disclosure and receivers' decisions.
This would provide an introduction to Kamenica–Gentzkow (2011), “Bayesian Persuasion.”

## Idea: conflicting interests and advice

Explore how differences in the sender's and receiver's objectives affect communication.
We envisage a case based on Crawford–Sobel (1982), “Strategic Information Transmission.”

 A demo and seminar activities are topics for future consideration.
